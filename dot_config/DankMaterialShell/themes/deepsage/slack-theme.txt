Deep Sage — Slack sidebar themes
================================
Derived from theme.json in this directory (same palette as the nvim
"deepsage" colorscheme and the cava gradient).

Paste into Slack: Preferences -> Themes -> scroll to the bottom ->
paste into the "copy and paste a theme" box. Recent desktop clients take
all 10 values; older ones take the first 8 and ignore the rest.

Slot order:
  1 Column BG        6 Text Color
  2 Menu BG Hover    7 Active Presence
  3 Active Item      8 Mention Badge
  4 Active Item Text 9 Top Nav BG
  5 Hover Item      10 Top Nav Text

Dark (flavor "sage" + "deep" accent) — pair with Slack appearance: Dark
#121915,#1E2922,#3A4F3C,#DCE6DF,#1B251F,#D7E2D9,#8BBF8A,#C85C55,#1B251F,#D7E2D9

  Mention badge is #C85C55 rather than the theme's error #E07A7A so that
  Slack's white badge text stays legible (4.1:1 instead of 2.9:1). Swap in
  #E07A7A for a more vivid badge at the cost of that contrast.

Light (flavor "sage-light" + "bright" accent) — pair with appearance: Light
#EEF3EE,#E3E9E3,#C3D3C2,#19231C,#E1E9E2,#1D2820,#4D7A4F,#C4453F,#E1E9E2,#1D2820

Note: a Slack custom theme only recolors the sidebar and top nav; the
message pane follows Slack's own light/dark appearance setting.
